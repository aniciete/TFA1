## Platform

web

## Users

Students, instructors, and retail cashiers reviewing the four-page architectural baseline of the CodeIgniter 4 POS system.

## Product Purpose

POS 4-Page Foundations (TFA1) is the initial CodeIgniter 4 application in the IT0049 Web Systems series (From Zero to Four Pages: Home, Customers, Users, About). Success means clean MVC routing, modular layouts, and self-hosted typography.

## Positioning

The foundational baseline of the IT0049 POS curriculum.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Backend**: CodeIgniter 4 with PHP 8.2+.
- **Views**: Four modular PHP views connected through a common layout.
- **Design System**: Bright-Market artisan ledger.
